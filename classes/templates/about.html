About Us

The Virtual Museum

A university story, kept alive.

The LSPU Heritage Museum is a student-developed virtual space where visitors can explore the university's history through campus exhibitions, photographs, historical milestones, and collected information.

It brings the four regular campuses together in one accessible experience and encourages students, alumni, and visitors to appreciate the people and events that helped shape the institution.

1952

The year LSPU traces its beginnings to Baybay Provincial High School in Siniloan, Laguna.

Our Purpose

Why this museum exists

To make LSPU history easier to discover, understand, and share through an engaging digital exhibition.

Our Objectives

  • Present the university's history and campus milestones.
  • Organize historical information and credited photographs in one place.
  • Provide an interactive learning experience for students and visitors.
  • Promote appreciation of LSPU's institutional and local heritage.

Our Direction

Mission & Vision

Mission

To preserve and present LSPU's history through reliable information, meaningful exhibits, and accessible digital storytelling.

Vision

To become a useful virtual heritage space that inspires every visitor to value the university's past and take part in its continuing story.

The Project Team

Meet the people behind the museum.

This virtual museum is developed as an academic project by a team of six student developers. Together, the team brings research, design, and web development into one digital space for sharing LSPU heritage.

Our goal is to make the university's stories easier to explore and appreciate.
