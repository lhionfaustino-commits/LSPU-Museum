The story of a university,

Laguna State Polytechnic University began in 1952 as a provincial high school beside Laguna de Bay. Today it has four regular campuses — Siniloan, Santa Cruz, Los Baños and San Pablo City. Walk into any of the four galleries below to follow its history.
